## 📖 About

Personal study notes for **Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras & TensorFlow (3rd Edition)** by Aurélien Géron. The notes come in **two versions**: an **Arabic** version (Arabic explanation with English technical terms) and a full **English** version. Both are organized chapter by chapter, with equations, diagrams and review questions. They are meant for revision, not as a replacement for the book.

## 🧭 How to Use

1. Pick your language (Arabic or English), read the chapter in the book first, then open the matching PDF to revise.
2. Use the review questions at the end of each PDF to test yourself.
3. Found a mistake or something unclear? Open an **Issue** and I will fix it.
